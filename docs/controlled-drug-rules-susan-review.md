## Proposed rules to approve or edit

| Event | Proposed practice rule | What the second person confirms |
|---|---|---|
| Receive stock | Record the actual receipt, supplier evidence, product, concentration, lot, expiration, container and quantity. A second authorized employee checks it before the stock becomes available. | Personally checked the received stock and source documents; not merely reviewed a typed quantity. |
| Administer to a patient or dispense | The authorized performer records the event promptly against the patient and source container. A different authorized employee reviews the record by the end of the next staffed business day. Late reviews create an escalation task. | Reviewed the recorded patient, drug, quantity, units, prescriber/performer, time and clinical link. This is **record review**, not a claim of observing administration. |
| Medication prescribed externally | Save the prescription and clinical record; do not subtract clinic stock. | Any applicable prescribing review is separate from stock handling. |
| Count / opening balance | Two authorized people independently count and reconcile before signing. Proposed routine frequency: each staffed day for clinic stock, plus every mobile-kit departure and return. | Each person counted the identified stock. An opening count establishes a physical baseline; it does not fabricate past administrations. |
| Move a mobile kit | Name the custodian and registered site controlling the stock; record containers, location, departure count and custody handoff. Reconcile at return. | Personally checked the handoff and count. A move within the practice is not a new purchase or a second clinical stock debit. |
| Correct a record | Preserve the original; append a reason and explicit corrected values. Require a different authorized reviewer. A quantity correction creates its own stock effect where appropriate. | Reviewed this correction’s exact version. An earlier signature does not approve the change. |
| Discrepancy, breakage or suspected loss | Record discovery time, observed facts and quantity; hold affected stock pending the responsible veterinarian’s assessment. No automatic “adjust to match.” Escalate immediately. | Reviewed the investigation and resolution, without delaying any required external notification. |
| Registered-location residual waste | Use a separately approved immediate-administration residual-waste procedure. Record the original draw, administered quantity and unusable residual without counting stock twice. Proposed practice preference: a second person personally observes waste. | Actual observation, time, quantity and method; a later record review cannot supply a missing observation. |
| Expired / unwanted clinic stock | Quarantine separately from usable stock. Use the approved reverse-distributor route or a reviewed destruction procedure. Preserve service/transfer evidence. | Reviewed the documented handoff, or personally witnessed destruction when that is the actual event; these are different attestations. |
| Routine client returns | Keep the controlled-drug return, restock and routine take-back workflow unavailable. Offer an approved external disposal resource once selected. | No ordinary return approval can turn client-held medication into usable clinic stock. |

**Proposed staffing rule:** each reviewer uses their own current account, must be authorized for the event, and cannot approve their own record review. A person performing destruction may be one of its two qualifying physical witnesses; this draft does not require a third person. Staff permissions and the reviewer roster remain unassigned.

**Proposed review queue:** “Awaiting record review,” “Awaiting physical witness evidence,” and “Discrepancy on hold” remain distinct. A past-due review alerts the responsible veterinarian; it never becomes automatically approved. An unavailable reviewer is recorded honestly and escalated, without backdating or borrowed credentials. A pending record review must not cause the system to hide or defer a physical event that has already occurred.

## Housecalls — proposed workflow and unresolved decision

Before departure, verify the kit’s documented custody, container-level quantities, approved storage/security arrangement and the responsible registered site. Record each patient event against its actual container and place/time. At return, reconcile remaining usable stock and separately accounted-for waste/custody evidence. If connectivity fails, preserve contemporaneous downtime records with the actual performer and event time, then reconcile them once; do not invent an electronic witness.

For a veterinarian working alone, subsequent review can check the record. It **cannot** retrospectively witness physical waste or destruction. No software exception will label an absent witness as present.

**Still unresolved: off-site residual waste.** Susan must select and document a lawful housecall procedure, including what happens when no qualifying second person is present, how any material is secured and transported if applicable, who may handle it, and its final disposition. This draft deliberately does not prescribe retention or transport of a used syringe, off-site destruction, a delayed witness, or a remote/video witness as an approved workaround. The mobile waste/destruction workflow remains unavailable until its specific legal and practical procedure is established. This gate does not authorize omission of an event that already happened.

**Incidental abandoned medication:** this is an exception incident requiring responsible-veterinarian direction, not permission to accept routine client returns. Keep it separate from usable stock and document the approved law-enforcement/DEA guidance and disposition.

## Legal constraints supporting the design

- Continuing records must be accurate and current, with actual receipt/transfer/destruction dates and attribution to the responsible registered location. Federal law does not universally require a perpetual inventory. The proposed daily counts and second-person review deadlines above are additional practice choices. The Form 41 exception for certain immediate-administration residuals expressly refers to the practitioner’s **registered location**; it is not a general housecall exemption. [21 CFR 1304.21](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.21).
- The applicable registrant destruction procedure requires personal observation by two employees; the destruction record includes their signatures. Transfer for destruction also has specific two-employee loading/unloading observation requirements. A software approval alone does not establish physical observation. Preserve the signed official record; the app is not represented as a certified electronic Form 41 filing/signature system. [21 CFR 1317.95](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1317#p-1317.95), [DEA Form 41 and instructions](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/21cfr_reports/surrend/41_form.pdf).
- Ordinary individual veterinary practitioners are not among the authorized collector categories for routine ultimate-user take-back. DEA distinguishes incidental abandoned medication and advises contacting law enforcement or the local DEA office when it cannot be returned to the ultimate user. [21 CFR 1317.30–.40](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1317#p-1317.30), [DEA disposal guidance](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/faq/disp-destr-faq.html).
- Theft or significant-loss reporting cannot wait for the app’s second approval: the responsible registrant assesses reportability and applicable deadlines, including written notice within one business day of discovery and electronic Form 106 within 45 days. [21 CFR 1301.76(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1301/subject-group-ECFRa7ff8142033a7a2/section-1301.76).
