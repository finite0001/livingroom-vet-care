# Clinic and housecall scheduling

The schedule supports a day or seven-day view in America/Denver. Staff book a household's patient, assigned staff member, duration, visit reason, location, address snapshot, optional shared resource, travel buffers and reminder offsets. Clinic visits use 2619 Spruce Street, Boulder, CO. Housecalls can open their destination in Google Maps. Assignment means an active staff account; it does not certify clinical credentials.

Each day with booked or confirmed housecalls now offers **Plan housecall route**. Select one assigned staff member to see that day's planned visits in chronological order, including clinic appointments between housecalls, with a start and return at 2619 Spruce Street. The itinerary uses saved appointment addresses; it does not substitute the household's current address or skip an earlier booked visit based on the clock. Completed, canceled and no-show appointments are excluded. Unassigned housecalls are identified for assignment. The route panel shows each stop's busy block, visit window, gap before the next busy block, and planned-hours warnings for staff review.

Each route leg opens driving directions only when staff choose its link. URLs contain the two saved addresses, not patient names or clinical details. Directions are not route optimization or a calculated travel allowance; staff still review their manual buffers. Missing addresses block route links, overlong URLs are not truncated, and incomplete/unknown schedule counts disable route planning. Refreshing temporarily removes directions links while preserving a still-valid staff selection and expanded route. The [Google Maps URL contract](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started) supports these address-based links without an API key. No embedded map, provider call, appointment write or additional subscription is added.

Household searches return at most 50 name matches and retain the selected household outside the search results. Patients are loaded for the selected household. Important historical problems and legacy allergy text appear during booking; unavailable alert data prevents saving until retried.

`save_appointment` authenticates the actor and serializes schedule writes with a transaction lock. It checks patient/household membership, active assignments, durations and overlap (including travel buffers and shared resources). Edits require the expected version; conflicts retain the editor's draft. Audit events preserve changes. Archived or deceased patients cannot receive new bookings; existing appointments can still be canceled.

Each saved revision invalidates pending reminders for the previous revision and creates jobs for the selected offsets (default 48 and 24 hours). Offsets already in the past are skipped. Cancellation and patient inactivity invalidate pending jobs. These jobs are not evidence of delivery: a durable dispatcher, consent/suppression rules and provider acceptance are separate work. Staff cannot write a sent status directly.

Validation includes SQL assertions, focused time/reminder and route-helper unit tests, and a browser scenario covering housecall details, alerts, conflict draft retention and selected patient retention after a new household search. Browser calls are mocked. Recurring availability, hosted booking acceptance and actual reminder delivery remain commercial-readiness gates.
