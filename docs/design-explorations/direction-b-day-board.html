What Direction B changes, and why (from the swarm audit)

  • Color = status, everywhere: booked is always blue, on site always terracotta, done always green — learnable in one shift, unlike today’s identical gray cards.
  • The day is a pipeline, not a dialog hunt: check-in is a one-tap move between columns instead of opening a 15-field editor to change a status dropdown.
  • Failures shout: the audit found failed sends and unmatched inquiries are nearly invisible (no nav badge, buried pages). Here they get a permanent severity-sorted queue with one button each.
  • Nav grouped by job-to-be-done: “Today / Records / Practice” replaces the flat 10-item Workspace list the audit flagged as day-one-hostile.
  • Jargon translated: “SLA compliance” becomes “Reply-time goal 94%”, counts come with plain sub-labels, no UUIDs or “outbox” vocabulary.
  • ⌘K search up front: visible in the sidebar, not a 32px mystery icon.
