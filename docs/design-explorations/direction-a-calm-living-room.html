What Direction A changes, and why (from the swarm audit)

  • Brand tokens actually used: cream, terracotta, forest, warm shadows — the audit found these existed in index.css but went unused; the hub read as generic shadcn.
  • “What do I do right now?” answered above the fold: the current home shows six identical count cards; here the next visit, its address, and one primary action lead the page.
  • Urgent things look urgent: failed messages and due items get warm color + one clear action instead of identical gray tiles. “All caught up” states say so explicitly.
  • Plain words: “Messages” not “Communication,” “house call” labels, no UUIDs, no “outbox” jargon — day-one readable.
  • Schedule reads at a glance: big 12-hour times, grouped Morning/Afternoon, colored status chips, one-tap Check in / Complete instead of the hidden status-dropdown edit dialog.
  • Search promises what it delivers (clients and pets) with a visible ⌘K hint — the audit found the placeholder promises pets but the query only searches clients.
