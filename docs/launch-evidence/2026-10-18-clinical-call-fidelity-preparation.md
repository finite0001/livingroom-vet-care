## Delivered safeguards

- Export creates a new private evidence directory and never overwrites existing material. Import requires exactly one result for each frozen case, with matching bundle and request hashes.
- Every complete HTTP body is preserved as canonical base64 and hashed before decoding. Invalid UTF8, refusal, non-success HTTP, malformed JSON, transport errors and oversized original responses remain failed outcomes. Parsing uses the existing production response/citation contract and original 512,000-byte HTTP limit.
- The readable Markdown and JSON review packets retain complete source segments, each whole claim with its own supports, selected-patient SOAP, flags, mandatory meanings and forbidden inferences. Hostile backtick content cannot break out of its evidence fence; 150,000 separated runs do not overflow function arguments.
- Every claim dimension, individual support and inventory fact requires an explicit named review with rationale and permitted evidence references. Pending, missing, duplicate or mismatched decisions fail closed. Changing raw bytes invalidates a completed review. Any structural or reviewed semantic failure prevents a fidelity pass.

Ten intentionally false synthetic outputs pass quotation/citation structure. They demonstrate the validator's boundary: source membership does not establish clinical entailment. They are engineering negative controls, not provider responses or a completed clinical evaluation. Other-pet history may be omitted entirely; if retained it must remain correctly attributed and outside the selected-patient SOAP. Speaker metadata need not become clinical prose.
