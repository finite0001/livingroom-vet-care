# Vaccine and rabies certificates — implementation and acceptance contract

Implemented as a local, reviewable module. No real credentials, signatures, phone numbers, or license numbers are seeded. No certificate is emailed, uploaded, or issued during migration. Dr. Susan Edler must accept the form, signature workflow, supervision attestation, and local-use policy before any issuer is activated. This is a custom certificate, not a claim of official NASPHV form approval or automatic legal acceptance.

## Primary-source research (reviewed September 12, 2026)

- [City of Boulder Voice and Sight program](https://bouldercolorado.gov/services/voice-and-sight-program) lists the certificate title, veterinary letterhead, patient identity and birth date, owner contact information, administration and next-due dates, lot/serial number, and tag number as useful submitted-certificate contents. Its acceptance process is specific to that program; a tag image alone is insufficient.
- [Boulder County Ordinance 2022-8, section 3(b), PDF page 5](https://assets.bouldercounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ordinance-2022-08-animal-control.pdf) specifies owner name/address/home and business phones; administering or supervising veterinarian name/address; the actual administrator when different; animal breed/age/color/name/sex/sterilization; vaccination and expiration dates; vaccine type, lot, manufacturer; and dog tag number. Its applicability language covers unincorporated county and permits application in contracting municipalities. Do not assume every county rule automatically applies inside the city.
- [Colorado official 2024 Title 25, section 25-4-607(1)(a)](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/images/olls/crs2024-title-25.pdf) and the [enacted 2022 amendments, section 29](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2022A/bills/sl/2022a_sl_442.pdf) identify the administering person and place training assurance on the signing veterinarian. This implementation captures an explicit named administrator and veterinarian attestation; it does not determine whether a particular delegation arrangement is lawful. The official 2025 consolidated PDF could not be retrieved in this research session; current law and local acceptance need clinician/operator confirmation before activation.
- [NASPHV rabies resources and certificate](https://nasphv.org/site_page.cfm?pk_association_webpage=28173&pk_association_webpage_menu=12517) provide the primary veterinary reference. The linked certificate distinguishes product duration from next vaccination due and includes product/manufacturer, lot, veterinarian license/signature, patient/owner identification, and clinic details. The page identifies the 2016 compendium as current and under revision. This module does not implement clinical interval rules.

## Authority and activation

`certificate_issuers` is a private operator-managed credential registry. Authenticated staff and `service_role` have SELECT only; no enrollment RPC exists. A trusted database operator must verify actual current Colorado licensure, record a verification reference/time, record Dr. Edler's clinical acceptance, and configure the real practice name/address/phone. The registry starts empty and inactive by default. Never fill unknown values with invented placeholders. Operator credential changes are audited; revoke `active` promptly if authorization changes.

Issuance requires active staff membership, canonical `DVM` role, an active registry entry, nonexpired CO license, and verification/acceptance timestamps not in the future. ADMIN or a self-described name alone is insufficient. The entered signature must exactly match the verified issuer name and `p_attest_review` must be true. This is an explicit authenticated electronic signature action, not automatic signing on export. The UI must display the full attestation before that action; do not pre-check consent or submit on preview.

For rabies, the treatment's frozen veterinarian name and license must match the issuer. External imported history is never repackaged as a new practice-administered rabies certificate: attach the original external certificate. Other legitimate replacement workflows need explicit clinical approval before extending this restriction.

## RPC sequence

1. Collect rabies-only `p_details` (below), or `{}` for general history. Call `preview_vaccine_certificate(p_pet_id uuid, p_kind text, p_rabies_treatment_id uuid, p_details jsonb)` with `p_kind = 'rabies' | 'vaccine_history'`. The issuer reviews the entire returned JSON snapshot, including patient/owner identity and every due date. Preview creates no certificate and applies no signature.
2. Call `issue_vaccine_certificate(p_id uuid, p_pet_id uuid, p_kind text, p_rabies_treatment_id uuid, p_details jsonb, p_reviewed_snapshot jsonb, p_signature_name text, p_attest_review boolean, p_replaces_id uuid = null, p_reason text = null)`. Retain the UUID and complete original payload through ambiguous failures. Repeated identical requests return the same row; altered payload/actor is rejected. `40001` means the preview changed and must be reviewed again. The server rebuilds the snapshot from actual records; arbitrary submitted snapshots cannot be certified.
3. Immediately before displaying/printing, call `read_vaccine_certificate(p_id uuid)`. It returns `{certificate, events}` in one statement, omits internal request data, and requires active staff. Never substitute an empty event list on a read error. Certificate events are authoritative invalidation history.
4. Pass the returned issued row and events to `renderVaccineCertificate(certificate, events)` from `src/hub/features/certificates/print.ts`. The function returns deterministic, escaped, self-contained print HTML. A null status lookup throws. Any event visibly invalidates the copy. The renderer never reads current patient, catalog, practice, or owner records; no network assets, automatic signing, or send action occurs. Browser Print → Save as PDF is supported; stored PDF generation and patient-document attachment are later integration work.
5. For a correction, create a new UUID and review a fresh preview with `p_replaces_id` and a nonempty reason. The original receives an append-only `superseded` event linking the replacement. A second successor for that original is rejected; follow the successor chain. `void_vaccine_certificate(p_id uuid, p_certificate_id uuid, p_reason text)` appends an idempotent reasoned void event. No update/delete operation is permitted on issued certificates or events.

`patient_treatment_corrections` automatically appends `treatment_corrected` events to every linked certificate in the same transaction. Source treatment rows are locked during issue using the same locks as corrections, preventing a correction from silently missing a concurrent certificate. Copies already downloaded cannot be remotely altered; certificate IDs and the current event history support later verification. There is no public verification URL yet.

## Snapshot and required details

Snapshot version 1 contains patient identity (including birth-date precision), owner identity/contact/address, issuer credentials and clinic contact, frozen vaccine administrations, and reviewed supplemental fields. Administration dates use America/Denver. Each vaccine includes product, manufacturer, lot, lot expiry, dose, route, site, administering/supervising veterinarian/license, administration date, recorded next due date, record origin, and age at administration. The issue timestamp is stored separately. Patient DOB, age, microchip, or a due date are never fabricated.

Rabies requires a known/estimated birth date explicitly labeled with its precision, breed/color, sex/sterilization, owner name/address/primary phone, a live uncorrected vaccine record, manufacturer/lot, lot expiry valid on administration, site, and an existing clinician-entered next due date. Supplemental string fields:

- `administrator`: actual administering person's name, distinct from the data-entry actor.
- `rabies_tag_number`: required for dogs; available for other species.
- `usda_duration`: `1 year`, `3 years`, or `other licensed duration` as reviewed against the actual product. This label never calculates the clinical due date; provide the specific duration in `vaccine_type` when using the third option.
- `vaccine_type`: reviewed vaccine formulation/type, with actual licensed duration if nonstandard.
- `size_description`: reviewed weight or size description, never silently taken from an old weight.
- `initial_or_booster`: `initial` or `booster`, explicitly reviewed.
- `owner_business_phone`, or boolean `owner_business_phone_unavailable: true` to distinguish unavailable information from omission. Primary owner phone remains required.
- Boolean `supervision_attested: true` requires the signer to confirm the named administrator and relevant supervision/training in the displayed signature attestation.

General certificates include **all uncorrected vaccine records in the reviewed snapshot**, preserving all their recorded due dates and clearly identifying imported history. A missing historical due date prints “Not recorded — no due date certified.” These are history certificates, not an inferred current vaccine-status/antigen schedule, and not rabies certificates. A later vaccine administration does not retroactively change a prior history snapshot. A product/antigen model and clinician-approved current-due selection remain separate work.
