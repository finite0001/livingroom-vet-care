# Resumable global reconciliation preparation

The maximum-volume experiment verified five million observations but timed out in the full projection under the ten-second budget. Keep the full objective: complete counts, operational resolutions, frozen reviewed reports, and supervised acceptance. Do not truncate, sample, extend the timeout as a substitute, or label partially prepared totals complete.

## Implementation sequence

1. Bound observation extraction by exact owned terminal binding and source-page interval before joins and evidence hashing. Preserve canonical occurrence hashes. Maximum twenty pages per request; deterministic page/ordinal/snapshot ordering. This is the current local increment, not a completed report workflow.
2. Add durable owner-scoped preparation identity, exact manifest/resolved-scope/binding digests, fixed source-page inventory, explicit progress and immutable chunk receipts. Replays must compare input hashes and return the same committed result. A replaced binding, changed inventory, missing page, unauthorized actor, or incompatible predecessor is not successful empty completion.
3. Adapt approval/native/context projections to the bounded observation input. Do not invoke a full-manifest helper then filter its result. Deduplicate receipt and native identities across persisted chunks before final counts; never sum per-chunk distinct counts.
4. Establish a consistency protocol across chunk transactions before publication. Inventory every mutable dependency (source heads, mappings/households/local versions, approval/supersession/extraction ledgers, capture status, scan/attempt/binding changes, and administrator activity). Use authoritative transactional change detection or an equivalently proven snapshot mechanism. A source-only watermark is insufficient. Changed evidence must invalidate/restart or require explicit refreshed review; timestamps alone cannot prove consistency.
5. Finalize only a complete contiguous chunk set with exact expected membership and hashes. A prepared report records what was examined and when; it does not assert source coverage, live provider cutoff, clinical approval, or cutover acceptance.
6. Add public authorization rechecks and operator recovery UI, followed by resolution/version linking and frozen acceptance. Preserve existing routes, buttons and review drill-downs.

## Required evidence

- First, middle, and final windows over all resource observation ledgers; canonical hash parity; no overlap/omission; invalid-window and foreign/inactive/replaced-binding denial.
- Five-million-observation dataset: bounded chunk latency, complete eventual traversal, global deduplication and bounded finalization. Fast first/last chunks alone are not full-scale report acceptance.
- Crash/retry/lost-response recovery and concurrent preparation requests.
- Mutations during preparation, finalization, review and acceptance; all dependency categories must be covered by the consistency protocol.
- No clinical/provider/delivery writes; private capture work stays owner-only; shared approved history remains visible.

Hosted backends remain unchanged. The existing full projection is a correctness comparison for bounded fixtures, not the production solution for maximum-volume manifests.
