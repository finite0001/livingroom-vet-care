## Design decisions

Use the existing stock ledger plus exact immutable controlled-event associations. Do not create a separately editable drug balance. A movement has one association, possibly split across multiple individually identified containers; allocation quantities total the movement exactly. Returned/quarantined custody has a separate association because it is already outside available stock. Anesthesia references the treatment/movement it already creates; no second debit or charge.

Store recorded/server time separately from observed physical time. Preserve current writer timestamp semantics and exact committed receipts. Opening inventory is a dated physical baseline, not invented historical administration. Baseline reconciliation is visible and included in opening once. Keep numeric(14,3), exact decimal strings and versioned concentration/package provenance; reject unsupported precision instead of rounding.

Distinguish `secondary_review` from `physical_witness`. A later review cannot attest personal observation. Ordinary dual-review frequency/deadlines are commissioned practice choices. Registered-location stock destruction requires two actual employee observers plus signed official Form41 evidence; an app hash or later second-login click does not establish that form's signature validity. Actual performer/recorder may be one of those two observers where permitted; do not invent a mandatory third witness. A separate independent secondary reviewer, if required by practice policy, is another role rather than a redefinition of the legal witness requirement.

Routine controlled client-return intake/restock/disposal is blocked by default; the existing ordinary-medication return APIs do not grant collection authority. Preserve an accurately labeled exceptional/abandoned custody incident only through a separately reviewed procedure, never as usable stock or routine take-back. Supplier returns, reverse-distributor handoff, actual destruction, residual immediate-administration waste and unexplained loss are distinct subtypes. External registrant distribution, procurement/CSOS signing, Form106 filing, PDMP and EPCS are not activated by this package. Mobile residual-waste handling remains blocked until the registered-location wording and actual practice SOP have been resolved.

Retain immutable evidence without destructive purge. Federal register minimum and Colorado patient-record retention use different clocks; linked clinical evidence survives the applicable longer period and holds. Printable ScheduleII inventory/register and separately retrievable III–V evidence are required outputs. Recheck the current Colorado consolidated rule PDF and applicable 2026 laws before operational sign-off: the report documents that retrieval limitation and future-effective 2027 material rather than presenting older text as current certification.
