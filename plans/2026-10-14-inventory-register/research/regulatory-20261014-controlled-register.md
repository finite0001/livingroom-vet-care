# Controlled-drug register: federal and Colorado requirements

Research date / retrieval date: **2026-10-14**. Scope: Living Room Vet's own Boulder, Colorado practice, including housecalls. Research only; no account access, filings, deployments, or code audit. Read project AGENTS.md, shared-memory LRV notes, stock-boundary.md and phase-03-controlled-register.md. The repository's 186-migration baseline is engineering context, not proof of legal readiness. No registration, license, authorized collector status, supplier account, destruction service, or PDMP account has been verified.

**Recommendation:** implement event-specific records and two distinct attestations: **review of a record** and **personal observation of a physical event**. Secondary approval on every transaction is a practice policy. Certain destruction activities expressly require two employees' physical observation and signatures; clicking approval later cannot establish that observation. Preserve actual events promptly even while review is pending. A controlled client return must not enter the ordinary restock/disposal workflow merely because the inventory implementation already supports returns.

## Implementation consequence matrix

“Required” below means supported by the cited provisions, subject to the actual registrant/activity. “Design” means an implementation/control recommendation, not a claimed statutory mandate.

| Event / concern | Legal requirement or limit | Consequence for this plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope of records | Federal direct-dispensing records are required. The administration exception does not apply when the individual practitioner regularly dispenses/administers and charges for the substances, including bundled professional charges. Prescribing-only has different federal record rules; Colorado still requires the clinical record. [§1304.03(b–d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFR64b4002fc681198/section-1304.03) | Distinguish **prescribe externally**, **dispense to client**, and **administer**. An outside-pharmacy prescription must not debit clinic stock. Do not assume an exemption for ordinary paid veterinary administration. |
| Receipts | Record drug name, finished form/strength, units or volume/container, quantity and actual receipt date; supplier name, address and registration number. [§1304.22(a)(2)(i),(ii),(iv),(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.22) | Store supplier identity snapshot and receipt evidence, not only a product/lot balance. Lot, expiry, unique container identity and acquisition-document linkage are useful additional controls. |
| Administration / dispensing | Record date, recipient name/address, units or volume, and written/typewritten name or initials of the dispensing/administering individual. [§1304.22(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.22) | Preserve owner/recipient address plus animal identity, responsible veterinarian, actual operator and clinical link. Separate stock volume from clinical dose and concentration. The federal rule does not mandate a second signer for every ordinary administration or dispense. |
| Clinical details | Colorado requires accurate, unalterable records explaining assessment, diagnosis, treatment and prescribed drugs/dosages; rule 1.13 adds responsible veterinarian, owner/patient identifiers, treatment strength/quantity/frequency and anesthesia agents/route/strength/monitoring/anesthetist. [C.R.S. §12-315-119(3)(b)](https://olls.info/crs/crs2025-title-12.htm), [official rule 1.13, pp.25–26, with version limits below](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=12180&fileName=4%20CCR%20727-1) | A register entry is not the entire patient record. Link immutable clinical evidence rather than treating a generic inventory reason as adequate clinical context. |
| Inventory / opening count | Initial inventory when first engaging in the activity, including a zero-stock record where appropriate; new inventory at least every two years, and on the effective date of newly scheduling a drug held. Include drug/form/strength, volume/units per commercial container, container count, actual count and opening/close-of-business designation. Schedule II opened containers need exact count/measurement; III–V may be estimated unless over 1,000 tablets/capsules. Include expired/damaged stock awaiting disposal and stock under the registrant's control elsewhere. [§1304.11(a–e)(6)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFR9944e94ba5f1eb0/section-1304.11) | Create preserved physical inventory snapshots with count method and quarantined stock. A software cutover count does not replace an earlier legally required initial/biennial inventory or manufacture missing history. |
| Perpetual balance / routine counts | Federal continuing records must be current, complete and accurate, but §1304.21(a) expressly says a perpetual inventory is not required. [§1304.21](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.21) | Running container balances, daily/end-of-trip counts, dual counts and reconciliation holds are defensible practice controls. Do not present their chosen frequency as a universal DEA rule. |
| Corrections / unexplained adjustment | Complete/current records and Colorado unalterability apply; the reviewed ordinary-register rules do not specify a cryptographic hash, a universal correction form or a universal cosign deadline. [§1304.21](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.21), [§12-315-119](https://olls.info/crs/crs2025-title-12.htm) | **Design:** immutable originals plus dated attributable amendment, reason, original linkage and explicit quantity effect. Counts record observed discrepancies before any compensating adjustment. Never relabel an unexplained shortage as administration or waste. |
| Mobile stock / internal transfer | A registered veterinarian may transport/dispense in usual veterinary practice elsewhere in a state where licensed, if the destination is not another principal place of business/practice. Unregistered-location stock/records remain attributed to the controlling registered location. [§1301.12(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1301/section-1301.12), [§1304.11(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFR9944e94ba5f1eb0/section-1304.11), [§1304.21(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.21) | Model controlling registrant/location separately from bag/vehicle/housecall location and custodian. **Design:** one paired custody transfer and matching return, not unrelated adjustments; preserve total stock. Mobility authority does not automatically authorize a second fixed storage/practice site. |
| Other-registrant transfer | Distribution to another dispensing practitioner under §1307.11 requires eligible receiving registration, both parties' records, Schedule II order form and the annual 5% dosage-unit limit unless separately registered to distribute. [§1307.11](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1307#p-1307.11) | Separate external distribution from internal bag movement; leave external transfers disabled unless this is an actual practice need with reviewed authority and documentation. |
| Residual immediate-administration waste | §1304.21(e) exempts certain immediate-administration remainder destruction **at the practitioner's registered location** from Form 41; §1304.22(c) recording remains. DEA's manual distinguishes pharmaceutical wastage from office-stock disposal. [§1304.21(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.21), [DEA manual p.40](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/GDP/(DEA-DC-071)(EO-DEA226)_Practitioner's_Manual_(final).pdf) | Distinct residual-waste event linked to administration with amount and disposal details. Do not classify an expired vial or all remaining usable multidose stock as this exception. **Mobile residual disposal is unresolved:** the literal registered-location limitation must not silently be extended to housecalls. Obtain the practice's reviewed handling SOP before activating that path. |
| Stock destruction | On-site destruction under §1317.95(d) requires two employees to handle/observe handling and personally witness destruction through non-retrievability. §1304.21(e) requires complete Form 41 with both names/signatures. [§1317.95(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1317#p-1317.95(d)), [§1304.21(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFRed220d26113f6a5/section-1304.21) | This is **two employee witnesses**, not automatically “one DVM plus one RVT,” and not an owner/client witness. Store both actual observers, observation date/time, attestations and Form 41 evidence; role eligibility still requires review. A second-login review of a description alone is insufficient. |
| Destruction documents / service handoff | Form 41 records registrant, drug identifiers (NDC or DEA drug code), strength/form, package/partial/total quantities, date/location/method and two authorized employees' signed declarations. It is retained, not routinely submitted unless DEA requests it. [Official Form 41 and instructions](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/21cfr_reports/surrend/41_form.pdf). §1317.95(a–c) imposes event-specific two-employee loading/unloading/transport/observation controls; these are destruction rules, not general mobile-veterinary rules. [§1317.95](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1317#p-1317.95) | Support a documented reverse-distributor/service handoff separately from destruction performed by the practice. Do not mark a handoff as witnessed final destruction or auto-file forms. Confirm actual disposal method and service documents. |
| Return / recall to supplier | Registrant return/recall transactions require acquisition-style records; Schedule I/II return/recall uses an order form. [§1317.10](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1317#p-1317.10) | Store counterpart name/address/registration, quantities, date, return/recall reason and order/invoice linkage. No restock based solely on a financial credit. |
| Client returns / abandoned medication | Ultimate-user collection is limited to authorized collectors and law enforcement. Ordinary individual veterinarians are not in the authorized collector categories. DEA's 2023 guidance directs practitioners with abandoned medication that cannot be returned to the ultimate user to seek law-enforcement/local DEA guidance. [§§1317.30,.40](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1317#p-1317.30), [DEA disposal Q&A](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/faq/disp-destr-faq.html) | Default controlled client return **intake for restock/disposal: blocked**. An incidental custody incident must remain distinguishable from lawful return authority and usable stock. Do not offer “take back your controls” or have staff accept them as routine stock. No SaaS-style general collector module is needed. |
| Loss / breakage | Practitioners must give written theft/significant-loss notice within one business day of discovery and submit complete electronic Form 106 within 45 days. Significance depends on listed factors. [§1301.76(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1301/subject-group-ECFRa7ff8142033a7a2/section-1301.76). DEA distinguishes accounted-for witnessed breakage/spillage from unexplained loss. [DEA disposal Q&A, June 17, 2021](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/faq/disp-destr-faq.html) | Capture discovery time separately from physical/entry time, substance/quantity, investigation and report receipt. Human determines reportability; the register must not delay notice pending a second signature. A numeric adjustment is not a report. |
| Schedule II procurement | The purchaser records received container quantities/dates on its Form 222 copy; electronic-order receipts link to the original signed order. Preserve executed/unaccepted/defective forms and statements, with special separation/retrieval requirements. [§§1305.13(e),.17,.22(g),.27](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1305) | Attach original procurement evidence and recorded receipt details. A local invoice import is not CSOS and does not replace an executed Form 222/order. Keep ordering/signing outside this implementation. |
| Separation / retention / retrieval | Federal minimum is two years from each inventory/record. Schedule I/II inventories/records are separate; III–V separate or readily retrievable; individual practitioners follow these rules. Inventory is maintained at the registered location in written/typewritten/printed form. [§1304.04(a),(f),(g)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFR64b4002fc681198/section-1304.04), [§1304.11(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFR9944e94ba5f1eb0/section-1304.11) | Separate Schedule II register/export and printable inventory; allow inspection/copying at the registered site. Private backups, restore and readable export are engineering requirements supporting preservation. Do not silently purge linked clinical evidence. |
| Electronic approvals / certification | §1304.22(c) permits written/typewritten name or initials for ordinary dispense/admin records. EPCS has additional signed-record/audit/application requirements under §1304.06/Part 1311. [§1304.06](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1304/subject-group-ECFR64b4002fc681198/section-1304.06) | Distinct accounts, reauthentication, immutable version-bound attestations, access audit and no self-review are **design choices** here. Do not claim DEA software certification, EPCS readiness, or that an application hash alone meets any Form 41 signature requirement. Preserve a signed official Form 41 artifact until the electronic signing workflow has been reviewed for that use. |

## Colorado applicability, dates and limits

- **Clinical retention is not a two-year rolling window.** C.R.S. §12-315-119(3)(b) requires at least **three years after the patient's last medical examination** and a written storage/security/disposal plan. Linked clinical/register records should survive the applicable longer period and any hold. This is not proof that every unrelated stock receipt has a universal three-year state minimum. [Official OLLS 2025 Title 12, §12-315-119](https://olls.info/crs/crs2025-title-12.htm).
- **State controlled-substance baseline:** C.R.S. §18-18-306 directs persons registered under its Part 3 to federal record/inventory requirements and applicable additional rules. It does not establish that this practice possesses a particular registration. [Official OLLS Title 18, §18-18-306](https://olls.info/crs/crs2025-title-18.htm).
- **Pharmacy rules cannot be copied wholesale into an ordinary veterinary clinic.** The [current pharmacy-rule index](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/DisplayRule.do?action=ruleinfo&ruleId=2254) lists an August 21, 2026 correction. Pharmacy outlet/wholesaler/limited-animal-control-license provisions have different scopes. This research does not establish LRV as any such outlet; a historical pharmacy PDF is not a basis for extra veterinary witness/retention requirements.
- **PDMP is separate from a clinic drug register.** §12-280-403(2)(a) requires a licensed practitioner with a current DEA registration to maintain a PDMP account. §12-280-404(4)(a),(a.5) expressly excepts veterinarians from its opioid/benzodiazepine query mandates; pharmacy uploads under (2)(c) are not proof of a universal vet-dispensing upload duty. §12-280-404(3)(c) limits veterinary access to the stated suspected-client-substance-use/mistreatment context. No account or reporting integration was verified or commissioned. [Official OLLS Title 12, §§12-280-403–404](https://olls.info/crs/crs2025-title-12.htm). Do not infer a PDMP connector requirement from a controlled log, or claim a vet is exempt from all prescription limitations: §12-30-109's veterinarian definition changed again effective January 1, 2026.
- **Current veterinary rule provenance:** [SOS index](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/DisplayRule.do?action=ruleinfo&ruleId=2310) lists current effective **May 30, 2026**, adopted April 9, published May 10; [docket 2026-00059](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/eDocketDetails.do?trackingNum=2026-00059) and its adopted text concern rule 1.9 endorsement. Rule 1.13 record fields were read in the official October 15, 2025 consolidated PDF, pp.25–26; later indexed amendments are the [rule 1.20 repeal, effective January 30, 2026](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/eDocketDetails.do?trackingNum=2025-00531) and rule 1.9 changes. **Retrieval limitation:** the SOS current-index PDF controls did not expose a working current download URL to these tools. The current consolidated PDF must be obtained/rechecked before activation; this report does not mislabel the older PDF as current or import its repealed benzodiazepine rule.
- **Near-term change:** [HB26-1198](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/HB26-1198) was signed April 29, 2026; its session-law listing gives **January 1, 2027** as effective. [Docket 2026-00399](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/eDocketDetails.do?trackingNum=2026-00399) records adoption October 8, AGO requested October 9, but no effective/publication date as retrieved. Its [adopted rule 1.22(A)(4)](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/Upload/AGORequest/AdoptedRules02026-00399.docx) explicitly excludes controlled drugs from donation authority. Neither the pending rule nor the future statute justifies enabling controlled client returns now. If compounded controls are planned, recheck rule 1.21's final effective text and extra source/batch/BUD/disposition fields before activation.

Federal eCFR pages retrieved showed Title 21 current through October 13, 2026 for §§1304.04/.06/.11/.21/.22 and §1301.75; §§1304.03 and 1301.76 showed October 9. These are displayed currency dates, not each provision's effective date. Relevant source-note publication dates include September 9, 2014 disposal amendments, December 30, 2016 mobility amendments, September 30, 2019 Form 222 amendments, June 22, 2023 loss-report amendments, and February 5, 2026 EMS amendments. EMS-specific new duties are not veterinary-mobile rules. DEA's manual is the **2023 edition**, interpretive guidance rather than binding law; the official Form 41 retrieved is the form itself, not a verified E-signature approval. OLLS 2025 titles include future-effective 2026 text; they are not an exhaustive consolidation of all 2026 session laws. Verify applicable state/current consolidated text against actual products, registrant category and SOP before operational sign-off. No “compliant” or “certified” conclusion follows from this research.

## Two necessary practice decisions

1. **Which actual registrant and stock arrangement will operate?** Identify the responsible veterinarian/registration category and controlling registered location, clinical staff/authorized employees, actual controlled products/concentrations/units, mobile bags/vehicles and their storage/return pattern. Supply verified registration details only through private practice configuration; do not invent them in migrations or seed data.
2. **What does secondary approval attest, by event?** Decide which events require record review versus contemporaneous personal observation; eligible staff, timing/escalation, sole-veterinarian housecall handling, count frequency, and the actual residual-waste/expired-stock destruction or reverse-distributor SOP. Obtain specific confirmation for the unresolved off-site residual-waste procedure. These choices can be recorded while foundations remain unactivated; they must not be guessed into legal claims.
