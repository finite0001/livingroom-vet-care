# Controlled-register server event and review design — October 14, 2026

Design report only; no implementation, hosted change, vendor/device proof or clinical acceptance. Baseline supplied for this revision: `45496da4`, 186 migrations. The completed [stock-boundary audit](stock-boundary.md) is the writer inventory; its older statements that package/conversion metadata does not exist are superseded by released 5A1 (`20261014202235_supplier_product_identity.sql`). 5A1 is reviewed metadata, not physical receiving or controlled activation. Stock remains `numeric(14,3)` and canonical catalog units remain immutable. This design supplements [5C](../phase-03-controlled-register.md) and preserves all original 45 requirements, including N01/N02/N19/H11/H12 and real supplier/iPhone acceptance elsewhere.

The minimal safe design is one physical inventory ledger, mandatory server event links for every controlled movement, and a separate custody channel for drugs already outside available stock. Secondary review and physical witnessing are different attestations. A dormant schema is not a usable register.

## Private additive model

Use additive migrations; never modify historical migration files. Proposed names are design contracts, not claims that tables/functions exist. Tables may live in `public` to match this repository, with RLS enabled and all direct PUBLIC/anon/authenticated/service_role privileges revoked. Only narrow authenticated SECURITY DEFINER RPCs can read or change them; fixed search paths, owner-only helpers, bounded reads and immutable UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE guards are required. Preserve actor identities with restrictive FKs and immutable name/role/session snapshots without requiring old Auth sessions to remain live forever.

Container current location is derived from its events; a whole-container relocation within one existing lot need not fabricate stock movements. If quantity moves between different existing lot identities, use the paired stock transfer below. Do not mutate `inventory_lots.location` or turn 5A1 distributor/package identity into physical container identity. Container availability can be derived as reviewed opening allocation plus linked post-opening movements; its sum must equal the corresponding ordinary lot balance.

## State, opening and cutover

1. Prepare draft policy and proposed real locations/container labels without altering stock or declaring past events. A draft classified product stays visibly uncommissioned. The default installation has no active product, no synthetic opening count and no activation endpoint enabled.
2. Commission all writer hooks, reviews, recovery, corrections, counts, reporting and required evidence before allowing `opening_frozen`. Enabling the RPC requires an explicit supported coverage manifest; a boolean feature flag alone is insufficient. Record effective function definitions/signatures and trigger/ACL identities in that release manifest. Future migration changes to writers require a renewed coverage check.
3. Freeze the product through the exclusive entry barrier described below. Freeze blocks new physical available-stock and custody mutations for that product with a visible reason. Read-only receipt recovery remains available. Record a boundary token and per-lot baseline movement membership/head; do not define the opening set by client timestamps or UUID ordering. Existing concurrent writes must drain before the freeze commits.
4. Under that freeze, staff record dated observed real counts/provenance for each physical container/location and separately record any returned/quarantined custody quantities. Allocate every nonzero ordinary lot balance exactly to identified containers. Record a zero opening only for a real applicable container; do not invent a vial to make an old allocation restockable. A later legacy return with no valid destination container must remain custody-only or be visibly blocked from restocking until a valid reviewed destination exists.
5. A mismatch between physical count and ordinary lot balance opens an investigation. Do not activate by silently choosing either quantity. An authorized, independently reviewed baseline reconciliation may post one explicit signed ordinary adjustment and linked `opening_reconciliation` event while frozen. Preserve the pre-count balance, observed count, difference, reason, resolution and source movement IDs. The resulting opening snapshot already includes this adjustment; do not add it again as a post-opening running delta.
6. Verify opening allocations sum to the resulting ordinary balances, capture outstanding return custody through its existing replay, and independently attest the exact opening packet under the commissioned policy. Activate atomically with its boundary and opening receipt. Old movements remain historical inventory evidence, not fabricated administrations. New containers enter only through a controlled receipt or reviewed container allocation/transfer; old generic receipt cannot create an unregistered lot/container.

Historical clinical transcription still has no physical debit. Label it as historical evidence, outside the physical running ledger; it cannot establish opening inventory or pretend a physical event occurred during active operation.

## Exact event↔movement and custody consistency

Extend the common `inventory_guard` boundary and add DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED checks on movement INSERT and event/link/action INSERT. Existing native return constraints demonstrate movement-first/link-later transactions; retain those checks as well. ACLs are necessary but not the only protection.

- Every active/suspended/retired controlled-product movement must have exactly one immutable register association. No active-product lot is exempt because it predates activation or lacks a container. `opening_frozen` permits only the specific reviewed opening reconciliation; all other new physical changes fail.
- Movement and event checks agree on movement ID, lot→product, aggregate signed quantity, immutable canonical unit, authenticated actor, actual recorded movement timestamp, event subtype/source and exact container allocations. Each link line must identify a valid container for that same lot/product and contribute exactly once. Container allocations must total the movement, never exceed container availability, and cannot be reused by a second event. Restrict signs by explicit subtype and validate source business evidence, rather than accepting a free-form `adjust` reason.
- Distinguish physical time from `inventory_movements.created_at`, which currently means recorded/server time for several writers. Preserve existing treatment `administered_at`, native dispense/return server timestamps and anesthesia procedure window semantics. An explicit event `occurred_at` stores the observed physical time; movement link preserves the exact existing recorded timestamp. Do not retimestamp legacy movements or falsely equate the two times. Return's existing event/movement timestamp equality remains unchanged. Late entries carry both times and a reason; ordered ledger positions use commit serialization, not backdated physical time.
- The reverse constraint rejects an event claiming stock impact without its exact movement/source/action receipt, orphan links, unrelated treatment/native allocation IDs, invalid actor/time, reused container allocations or a forged saved receipt. Anesthesia references the same treatment and movement; its source link is checked before transaction commit and creates no second event/debit.
- Custody-only intake, disposal, retract-intake and retract-disposal require a second shared boundary on new `native_return_events` plus deferred source/event link checks. A movement trigger cannot cover them. Both v1 and v2 sources must have exact return-event/allocation/hash/actor/recorded-time associations. Restock/retract-restock require both custody replay linkage and ordinary movement linkage, with one event representing the combined transition.
- For controlled generic waste/verified count/return uses, require a structured purpose, source and reviewed context. Direct controlled `adjust_inventory` without that packet fails; clinical record annotation never implicitly reverses stock. Direct raw INSERT with staff-like JWT claims, a made-up event or arbitrary `set_config(...)` cannot satisfy admission + source + prepared-action + reverse constraints. Never use `current_setting('app.controlled_bypass',...)`, `session_replication_role` or a caller-editable GUC as application authorization.
- The BEFORE guard requires trusted entry admission for every new physical movement, including ordinary products, so a future uninstrumented writer fails visibly instead of racing cutover or silently bypassing coverage. It does not acquire the activation barrier late. Raw movement fixtures must use an explicit test-only setup path outside the production API, then prove production guards reject raw writes. An owner able to disable/replace triggers is outside application enforcement; migration/restore integrity checks detect that boundary.

Keep exact decimal strings end to end; check finite/range/precision before numeric(14,3) coercion and reject unsupported results. Released 5A1 conversion versions can supply reviewed provenance, but cannot increase physical precision. For example a conversion yielding a fourth decimal requires a different commissioned physical unit/model, not rounding. An event freezes the package/conversion version used; later metadata changes cannot reinterpret it.

## Prepared actions and lost acknowledgements

Expose narrow `prepare_controlled_action(actor, action_id, operation, request)`, `read_controlled_action(actor, action_id)`, `execute_controlled_action(actor, action_id)` and `cancel_controlled_action(actor, action_id)` plus bounded register/queue/report reads. Validation uses explicit typed/Zod API boundaries and private SQL validation. Suggested engineering bounds match 5A1 initially: 15-minute review expiry, 32 pending reviews per actor, 32KiB request ceiling, bounded allocations, 2-second lock/5-second statement budget; prove actual required allocation sizes before finalizing limits.

Prepare freezes actor/action UUID, canonical clinical request, controlled event context, expected policy/container/count/custody heads, source request and conversion versions. It performs no stock mutation. Execute revalidates fresh context and roles after waits, then calls the effective canonical writer exactly once and commits events/links/receipt atomically. Use the canonical operation UUID as root identity where possible; native dispense allocations retain their first-commit generated movement UUIDs in the saved receipt.

Existing generic RPCs remain useful for ordinary products. Their controlled branch requires the exact prepared packet and immutable review; direct invocation cannot skip preparation. Add compatible overloads/versioned RPCs or optional final context where needed; never change the request fingerprint for an already committed historical UUID. Strict-key JSON validators in treatment/anesthesia/native APIs must explicitly accept and validate the new field. If a native existing context hash changes, version the new request contract; do not rewrite an old receipt.

Recover the original owned committed receipt before current expiry/freshness/clinical-signing checks, while still enforcing current read authorization/live session. Changed UUID payload/actor fails; cancelled or expired uncommitted work cannot execute. A lost execute response leaves its original UUID/request pointer intact. Reload/actor-change reads the outcome without executing; a timeout or missing read result is not proof of no commit. Cancel serializes with execute: return the saved receipt if execution won, otherwise persist a cancellation tombstone even if preparation was never received. Never generate a replacement UUID automatically. A new legitimate session for the same actor may recover; record the executing session separately and require fresh preparation if uncommitted review's session policy is no longer valid. Another actor cannot adopt or execute that review; separately authorized clinical readers can see the committed register event.

Reuse the 5A1 pattern and current `next_visit_review_session()` as evidence of a real matched Auth session (checks `auth.sessions.user_id/not_after`), without expanding the unrelated operational-task protocol. The controlled helper must be invoked afresh after blocking waits and immediately before posting/signing; do not rely on a cached session UUID or STABLE result as a revocation recheck. No proposed role grants are activated by this report. Existing ordinary DVM/STAFF rules and native return safeguards remain minimum requirements; controlled permissions may narrow them under commissioned policy.

## Lock design and all required hooks

Do not blindly prepend a product/container lock inside one trigger. Existing treatment locks invoice/patient before product/lot, native fulfillment has slot/authorization locks, return v2 locks authorization before product/lot, and anesthesia locks its record before invoking treatment. A trigger acquiring a new exclusive control lock after a writer holds a lot can deadlock against activation waiting for that lot.

Use one reserved practice-level transaction advisory barrier: every outer stock/custody writer takes it **shared before any existing operation/invoice/authorization/anesthesia/lot lock**, then creates private current-transaction admission. Opening/policy activation/freeze transitions take it exclusive before any product/lot/container lock. Shared ordinary writers retain concurrency; infrequent exclusive commissioning drains them. Nested anesthesia→treatment acquisition is transaction-reentrant and must not upgrade the shared barrier. Reject unsupported isolation for these mutation RPCs (use READ COMMITTED); verify refreshed policy state after barrier waits so old snapshots cannot cross activation unnoticed. A prepared review never holds this barrier between HTTP requests.

Preserve each writer's current lock tail. Add container/position locks only **after all its product SHARE and sorted distinct lot UPDATE locks**, then lock affected containers/position heads in sorted UUID order and revalidate session/policy/availability. No path holding a container lock may later acquire a lot or clinical invoice/authorization lock. For adjust, resolve immutable lot→product without locking, then add product SHARE before lot UPDATE if policy checks need a product lock; re-read under locks. New multi-lot operations acquire all products sorted before all lots sorted before containers. Policy updates take exclusive barrier, avoiding an incompatible new per-policy lock acquired halfway through native paths. Witness-only work does not acquire stock/lot locks or invoke writers.

## Review, physical witnessing and resolution

A committed physical event immediately affects stock once. Its secondary review can remain visibly pending. A reviewer independently checks the recorded evidence using their own active authorized identity/live session; that attestation does not assert physical presence. A physical witness explicitly attests what they actually observed, with observation time and role. Record authenticated recorder separately from actual performer when these differ; a descriptive veterinarian name is not proof of a performing/signing identity. Require independent signers different from the recorder/performer under the commissioned rules and bind every signature to the exact event/hash/policy; never treat a name field, another user's open browser, a general DVM role or a later queue approval as physical witnessing. Whether a specific event needs pre-action approval, contemporaneous physical witnessing or later secondary review is a commissioned practice/legal policy, not inferred here.

Do not hide actual physical stock movement because review is outstanding. If policy requires pre-action permission, the workflow must obtain it before authorizing the action; if an action already occurred outside that workflow, capture it accurately as a late/exception entry with investigation and visible overdue attestation. A reviewer rejects/queries with an immutable reason; original stock remains as recorded until a separate authorized resolution. No rejected signature silently refunds stock. Corrections require their own appropriate review/witness; old attestations stay attached to old evidence and cannot satisfy the replacement event.

- **Metadata clarification:** append a linked amendment explaining the factual correction; no movement. Treatment/prescription correction artifacts remain their own source evidence and never create a stock reversal implicitly.
- **Quantity correction:** append the explicitly calculated stock delta and linked compensating event, citing original IDs, reason and count/source proof. Do not delete or replace an original debit; do not automatically reverse a full dose and debit it again. Preserve native return's specialized compensation links and shortage/hold rules.
- **Clinic/mobile transfer:** one prepared operation. Whole-container relocation within one lot posts a custody/location transition and zero available-stock delta. Quantity crossing distinct lot identities posts an atomic negative source movement plus positive destination movement tied to one transfer group; same product/unit/quantity and zero net product change are deferred constraints. Source availability and both container identities are checked under sorted locks; destination lot metadata is explicit and existing natural-key collision rules remain. Partial container movement identifies the destination container and exact remainder. No two unrelated adjustments.
- **Count:** freeze expected container/lot/custody head and exact balance, record observed quantity and physical count time, then finalize only if those heads remain unchanged. Movement after snapshot causes stale review, not automatic rebasing of an observed count. A mismatch opens an investigation with owner/evidence/hold. Final resolution may explain without changing stock or authorize one explicit delta. Use the union of native-return and controlled holds; neither system can clear the other's hold. Preserve existing permitted receipt/guarded compensation exceptions unless commissioned policy deliberately narrows them.
- **Returned/waste custody:** original dispense already debited available stock. Client return intake adds quarantined/return custody only; disposal subtracts that custody only. Never debit available stock again at disposal. Retraction is an immutable correction of custody evidence and may create a discrepancy, not proof that a physically disposed drug reappeared. Restock moves eligible custody into available stock once through existing native safeguards; retract-restock removes available stock once with the existing compensation path. Generic physical waste from still-available inventory does debit stock once and is a different subtype.

## Reports and proof

Bound report by product/container/location/date and explicit ledger boundary. Show reviewed opening, opening reconciliation separately, signed movement deltas, closing available balance, custody opening/transitions/closing, physical versus recorded times, exact units/conversion provenance, source IDs, performer, secondary reviewer and actual physical witness separately, pending/rejected/overdue attestations, correction/transfer links and unresolved discrepancies. Provide both chronological recorded ledger and observed-time views without changing totals. Historical inventory before opening is separately identified source evidence. An unresolved discrepancy must remain visible in export even if the physical running balance is nonnegative.

For each active lot: sum container openings + all linked post-boundary ordinary movement quantities equals ordinary inventory balance; aggregate container allocations equal every movement. Custody totals reconcile to native replay, not the available-stock ledger. Transfer pair sums to zero across lot identities; whole-container moves sum to zero by quantity and change location once. Opening baseline includes pre-boundary reconciliation exactly once. Export rows carry a stable position/key and signed exact decimal text; sanitize spreadsheet formula-leading cells. Retention/export access derives from the separately researched authoritative requirements and commissioned policy; do not enable destructive purge or claim regulatory certification.

## Small first engineering slice and activation remainder

**First slice C0, explicitly default-off:** one additive private schema/validator/immutable-link foundation, owned prepare/read/cancel recovery, denied execution for unsupported operations, and common movement/source-event constraints that enforce any future controlled association but have no active policy to operate. Hard-disable opening/activation and do not expose a usable-register label. The admission requirement for ordinary movements is not enabled in C0: ship it atomically with all entry hooks in C1, before any opening/activation can be enabled. C0 never creates an active policy or permits a controlled physical execute. Test ACLs, immutability, exact link consistency, own unknown-ACK/cancel semantics and populated scaffold restore. Do not add partial treatment-only hooks and imply complete coverage. This slice can be reviewed and installed without deciding practice witness permissions or entering physical opening inventory.

**Required before C0 can become an operational register:** implement early shared admission for every table-listed writer; controlled context and source-event enforcement in each; native v1 and v2 custody-only hooks; opening/freeze/activation with reviewed true counts; secondary review and any selected physical-witness rules; all correction/transfer/count/hold flows and bounded reports; exact stock/custody reconciliation and populated restore; true Auth/session, real two-user/browser, concurrency, stage-first and clinical/operational acceptance. A temporary unsupported controlled operation must fail before mutation. The activation RPC remains denied until all these are complete and practice policy is commissioned. This work does not replace supplier-import or real-camera requirements.

Only two practice-policy decisions are necessary to commission the complete flows:

1. Which actual products/concentrations/canonical units, individual container conventions and clinic/mobile locations enter the register, and who supplies the dated physical opening inventory and provenance?
2. For each event, who may perform it, independently review it or actually witness it; when is each attestation required; and who owns counts, discrepancies and escalation when a required second person is unavailable during a housecall?

The separate legal researcher determines authoritative constraints before these choices are activated. This design supplies no legal conclusion, new vendor interface or device/clinical acceptance claim.
