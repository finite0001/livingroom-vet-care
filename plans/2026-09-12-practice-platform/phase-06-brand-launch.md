# Phase 6 — brand, public site and two launches

Priority: brand discovery in phase 1; housecall site before late October; clinic launch early 2027. Status: planned. Context: [release schedule](release-schedule.md).

## Brand brief

Position Living Room Vet as calm, personal veterinary care connected to home, serving Boulder from a Spruce Street home base. Avoid making the public site imply the physical clinic is already open during the housecall launch.

Explore three logo directions: (1) warm residential, with subtle doorway/room geometry; (2) Boulder botanical, with restrained spruce/local references; (3) quiet contemporary wordmark, with a small distinctive animal/home symbol. Present concepts on signage, mobile header, invoice, vaccine certificate and avatar before choosing. Develop original identity rather than relying on a generic paw/medical-cross mark.

Deliver horizontal/stacked wordmark, small icon, monochrome/reversed variants, SVG/PDF/PNG exports, clear-space/minimum-size rules and documented font licenses. Build semantic color tokens for calm surfaces, readable text, actions and distinct clinical warnings; branding must not dilute alert contrast. Use owned/approved photography and accurate staff bios. Logo production is a subsequent design task, not completed by this plan.
