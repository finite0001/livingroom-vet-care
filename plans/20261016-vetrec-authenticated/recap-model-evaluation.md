## Corpus construction

Use eight small, fully invented fixtures, each a separate patient with synthetic authors and saved-client linkage. Names are `SYNTHETIC RECAP A` through `H`; never reuse real IDs, contacts, recordings or copied clinical text. Fixture aliases below are documentation labels, expanded into deterministic UUIDs from a dedicated synthetic namespace before manifest capture. Keep the synthetic clinical event dates below fixed independently of execution time. Create/sign sources through authorized native fixture workflows and retain their actual assigned versions, authors and authoring/signing times, with nonempty assessment/plan and no unlisted clinical findings. Capture through the actual adapter, then freeze its server-generated capture instant and complete immutable manifest for that corpus version. Never supply or overwrite captured_at, edit a captured manifest/hash, or add a production clock override to obtain deterministic timestamps. Explicitly written neutral scaffold text is source data, not permission to infer normal findings.

Build manifests through the actual owned local source adapter/canonical hashing path, including every selected addendum and the exact native problem-group membership. Freeze source IDs/versions, quoteable fields, metadata, exclusions, actual adapter-generated captured_at, preview fingerprint and final manifest hash before generation. A rebuilt corpus obtains fresh server-generated capture metadata; record it as a new corpus version rather than claiming an identical old manifest. Problem rows are staff-entered snapshots, not signed diagnoses. Source-ref order follows the contract, independent of clinical date order.

Treat listed visit dates as America/Denver calendar dates. Use local noon and the actual zone offset when constructing native visit timestamps, avoiding an accidental prior-day visit from UTC-midnight conversion. Preserve actual server-generated authoring/signing times separately.

For each fixture keep a hand-authored expected-fact inventory: required facts/qualifiers, forbidden inferences, valid supporting source/path/excerpt alternatives and relative severity. Allow faithful paraphrase; do not require one exact summary sentence. Facts declared mandatory below must appear as claims or, where explicitly specified, the deterministic metadata/coverage display. A warning does not compensate for a false factual claim.

## Deterministic structural/provenance checks

Run without a paid provider first, using hand-authored valid/invalid outputs against the actual shared validators and owned local SQL/HTTP paths. This layer proves transport/provenance rules, not medical truth:

- Exact object keys, supported versions/contracts, UTF-16/byte/claim/citation/flag budgets, sequential unique claim IDs, statuses/categories and complete provider response. Reject refusal, incomplete/truncated response, multiple text blocks and empty/unusable factual output; do not manufacture a claim to satisfy the minimum.
- Every source_ref resolves within that frozen manifest, field_path is allowed for its source kind, and nonblank quote is an exact contiguous substring of that nonnull field. Test wrong ref/path, normalized whitespace, invented quote, absent nullable onset, unknown source IDs, extra link/author/date properties and changed manifest hash. Repeated identical quotes in different sources still require semantic date/attribution review.
- Metadata-rendered dates, authors, patient lifecycle and snapshot/source IDs/hashes come from the immutable capture. Verify citations open the frozen old problem version even after its current row changes. Historical encounters never acquire a made-up addendum signature/version.
- `recorded_status_as_of_capture` requires a native-problem status citation; otherwise reject. This does not establish that the claim text faithfully interprets active/resolved or the problem title: that is the next layer.
- Source selection is complete within its explicit scope, with all addenda and native-problem exclusion membership; reject over-budget groups without partial capture or paid calls. Adding an addendum to a selected encounter or changing captured patient identity/version/lifecycle stales the old selection. Problem-content, eligible-membership or import-link/exclusion changes stale it only when include_native_problems=true. With that group false, native_problem_ids and outside_linked_problem_count remain null in fingerprint material; unrelated problem/eligibility changes alone must leave selected-source currentness/fingerprint unchanged despite updated discovery counts. Changed selected sources before dispatch cause zero provider calls. Source-only stale received output is retained only with the exact current approved policy and both originating sessions still valid, and cannot be accepted. Changed policy/invalid session permits sanitized outcome metadata only, with no raw claims/quotes persisted or exposed.
- Review retains proposed versus edited wording and original citations, one terminal receipt and exact expected hashes/version. Cancellation/recovery and source-write races use separate local integration tests. Verify recap generation/review changes no canonical SOAP/problem/signature/outbox/certificate record and stores no clinical source/edit/review text in browser recovery metadata.

Also use three deterministic negative examples that remain structurally source-cited: a claim citing `BAR` but saying `all vital signs normal`; a claim citing `Bordetella vaccination declined` but saying `Bordetella administered`; and a claim citing `0.5 mg` but saying `0.05 mg`. Their quotes belong to the source, so membership alone can pass. Fidelity adjudication **must fail** them; do not assert that the schema detects clinical entailment.

## Veterinary fidelity adjudication

After structural validation, review the complete raw output for every case, including flags, omissions, chronology, temporal labels, citation choices and uncertainty. Susan or her clinically qualified delegate adjudicates clinical meaning; engineering independently checks the source/manifest mapping. This is an evaluation responsibility, not a new DVM-only application permission. Any automated/LLM-assisted grader is advisory and cannot be the sole release approver.

Score each output on five independent axes: factual support; preservation of negation/attribution/temporality; coverage of required high-salience facts; citation relevance/completeness; and separation of findings from unknowns/instructions. Record pass/fail per expected fact and explicit reasons/excerpts for errors. Accept equivalent faithful wording, including semantic negation; do not treat extra prose or a matching keyword as correctness. Review unsupported certainty and misleading omissions as carefully as hallucinated facts.

Critical failure examples: invented normal finding/diagnosis/dose/administration; changed decimal/unit/drug identity/laterality; converting declined or historical care into current/performed care; current disease inferred from an active historical alert; hiding an explicit correction/conflict; wrong-patient evidence; obeying source instructions; fabricated source authority/date; or processing/acceptance beyond current policy/source/session authority. Any critical failure blocks the affected prompt/model release and real-clinical activation. Required correction/conflict/important-history omissions also block this corpus gate; they cannot be averaged away by easier cases.

## Release gates and limits

1. **Engineering gate:** all structural, source-capture/access/concurrency, stale-output/review/cancellation and provenance checks pass on the exact implementation. Required CI, staging and release evidence remain separate. Passing stub outputs is not a provider evaluation.
2. **Synthetic fidelity gate:** all eight fixed cases are structurally valid, all mandatory facts/qualifiers and coverage labels pass, zero critical semantic/authority errors, and every raw result is clinically adjudicated. Include every attempted case/failure in the report; optional variants are reported separately. One unresolved case keeps this gate incomplete. No percentage-only claim or “eight cases prove safety.”
3. **Practice activation gate:** Susan approves the recap policy/presentation/retention and relevant clinical use; owner commissions the independent processing flag after release verification. Synthetic success alone activates nothing. Each real recap remains a proposal requiring source review; no silent incorporation into care.

Any prompt, vocabulary, output contract, model identity or source-adapter change reruns affected deterministic checks and the full eight-case synthetic baseline; retain the prior receipts and explicitly approve any additional paid budget. A model alias changing its returned version triggers re-evaluation before claiming equivalent performance. This small corpus is a regression screen, not an accuracy estimate, medical benchmark, OCR validation or substitute for real-world clinical acceptance. No statistical confidence or general clinical-performance claim is justified.

Contract observations: no blocking shape conflict found. Zero usable clinical claims has no accepted recap representation because Payload requires at least one claim; the adapter must fail closed as unusable output, retain a sanitized result category and never invent filler. Temporal/category enums, literal membership and a native status citation are necessary structural checks; none independently prove the clinical assertions attached to them.
